Space is the next frontier: mining, orbital data centres, lunar bases. Our first foothold is the Moon, craters like Daedalus on the far side, holding water ice and minerals. But wheeled rovers get stuck on 30 degree walls, there is no radio contact with Earth, and it drops to minus 180.
